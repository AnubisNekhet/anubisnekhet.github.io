An Idiot's Guide to Cooking - Lazy Laziji

Macros

60 grams of protein per 100 gram of raw chicken breast, add like 100-120 calories per 200 grams of chicken to account for the spices and oil.
It's high protein and low calorie, that's all that matters

Requirements

  • Air Fryer
  • Chicken (Preferably breast)
    250 grams to 1 kilogram, per your requirements
  • Oil Spray
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper Powder
  • Onions
  • Green Bell Peppers
  • Corn Starch
  • Baking Powder
  • Chilli Flakes
  • Ginger & Garlic
  • Chillies
  • Sesame seeds
  • Szechuan Sauce (1 tablespoon per per 250g chicken)
  • Soy Sauce (2 tablespoons per per 250g chicken)
  • Ketchup (1 tablespoon per per 250g chicken)

Step 1: Chicken Prep

  • Follow the recipe in this guide to prep the chicken.

Step 2: Coating

  1. Once you've let your chicken sit, take it out and shake it rigorously. Drain out the stock and keep it in a bowl.
  2. Mix in corn starch and baking powder.
  3. Add chilli flakes to taste and mix.

Step 3: Air Frying

  1. Pre-heat your air-fryer to 200°C.
  2. Place your chicken in the air fryer.
  3. Spray oil on the chicken.
  4. Cook it for 5 minutes @ 200°C on one side.
  5. Once it's done cooking, flip it and spray oil.
  6. Cook it for 5 minutes @ 200°C on the other side.

Step 4: Cooking

  1. Dice your bell peppers and onions.
  2. Place a non-stick cooking pan on the stove, set it on low.
  3. Spray oil on the pan.
  4. Add the bell peppers.
  5. Once the bell peppers soften up, add the onions.
  6. Once the onions caramelize, add thinly chopped green chillies.
  7. Add the air-fryed coated chicken after 5 minutes.
  8. Add soy sauce and keep stirring.
  9. Add your Szechuan sauce and Ginger and Garlic.
  10. Add ketchup.
  11. Add some water to decrease the sauce's viscosity.
  12. Add your sesame seeds (Optionally chopped chives) on top.
