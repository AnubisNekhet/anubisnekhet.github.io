An Idiot's Guide to Cooking - Air Fried Chicken

Macros

60 grams per 100 gram of raw chicken breast, add like 100-120 calories per 200 grams of chicken to account for the spices and oil.
It's high protein and low calorie, that's all that matters

Requirements

  • Air Fryer
  • Silicone tray
  • Chicken (Preferably breast)
    250 grams to 1 kilogram, per your requirements
  • Oil Spray
  • Curd
    Like 100 grams at most
  • Lemons
    1 per 250-ish grams
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • Salt
  • Red Chilli Powder
  • Black Pepper Powder
  • Turmeric Powder
  • Onions
  • Green Bell Peppers
  • Garam Masala

Step 1: Chicken Prep

  1. Take your chicken and score it with a knife.
  2. Take your lemons and squeeze the juice onto the chicken.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and salt to taste
  4. Place it in your fridge for a minimum of 4 hours
    The citric acid from the lemon juice breaks down the muscle fibers so it's not as chewy when you cook it.

Step 2: Marination

  1. Once you've let your chicken sit, take it out and shake it rigorously.
  2. Dice some bell peppers and onions and place it into the bowl/meal-prep box.
  3. Curd (1 spoonful per 250 grams)
  4. Red Chilli Powder
    More for spiciness
  5. Turmeric
  6. Garam Masala
  7. Black Pepper Powder
  8. Mix well until you get a vibrant orange-red color.
    A light color means you've either added too much curd or too little spices.
    A yellow color means you've put in too much turmeric.
  9. Let it sit if you want the flavors to infuse in the chicken. 30 minutes should suffice.

Step 3: Air Frying

  1. Pre-heat your air-fryer to 200°C.
  2. Place your chicken and vegetables in a silicone tray.
  3. Spray oil on the chicken.
  4. Cook it for 10 minutes @ 200°C on one side.
  5. Once it's done cooking, flip it and spray oil.
  6. Cook it for 10 minutes @ 200°C on the other side.

Plate it with some mint chutney to make it more flavorful. You can meal prep this upto a kilogram; All you need is to air fry it to get some chicken that's easy to cook and not painful to eat.
