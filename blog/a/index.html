An Idiot's Guide to Infused Cooking

Requirements

  • Air Fryer/Convection Oven
  • Oregano
  • Butter
    My rule of thumb is usually 25 grams per 2 sticks of butter, you can experiment as you wish.
  • Induction cooktop
    You can use gas, but it's more cost-effective to simmer on an induction cooktop since you only need some heat in that step.

Step 1: Decarbing your oregano

  1. Preheat your oven/air fryer to 180°C.
  2. Decarb your oregano at 180°C for 16-18 minutes in an aluminium foil or parchment paper.
    Make sure to weight your oregano down beforehand so it does not get lifted due to all the hot air.

Step 2: Clarifying your butter

If you have an induction cooktop:
  1. Place a deep pan on your induction cooktop and fill it with water.
    Fill it upto the halfway mark.
  2. Get it to a boil on 800-1000 watts.
  3. Take your butter and place it in a metal bowl. Place it in the pan.
  4. Keep refilling the pan with water if you think the bowl is about to make direct contact with the bottom of the pan.
  5. When you see small bubbles forming in the butter, it's clarified enough to start the infusion process (This should usually take half an hour at most).

Step 3: Infusing your butter

  1. Take your decarbed oregano out of the air fryer/oven, crush it into a fine powder.
  2. Place your oregano in the bowl and mix properly.
  3. Bring your induction cooktop to 500 watts and let it simmer for a minimum of 4 hours
    More time spent infusing = Stronger extraction; I usually infuse for 7-8 hours.
  4. Keep refilling the pan with water if you think the bowl is about to make direct contact with the bottom of the pan.
  5. Keep stirring the bowl once every 10 minutes.
  6. Once you're done, wait for 3-5 minutes before the next step.

Step 4: Extraction

  1. Extract your butter into a mason jar (Preferable) by straining it through a cheesecloth or if your lazy enough like me, a strainer.
  2. Place your jar in the freezer overnight/until it solidifies completely.

Congratulations, you have a jarful of edible butter now! You can use this butter like everyday regular butter in cakes and even stuff like butter chicken. A spoonful of it should be enough to get you through the day.
